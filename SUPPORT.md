# Getting help

Three places, depending on what you need.

| You want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Ask how to use it, or what a number means | [Discussions](https://github.com/elementmerc/senbonzakura/discussions) |
| Report something broken | [Issues](https://github.com/elementmerc/senbonzakura/issues) |
| Report a security problem | ops@themalwarefiles.com, and see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

## Before you ask

The [documentation](https://elementmerc.github.io/senbonzakura) covers the method, the corpus and
its split, and every condition attached to every number this project has published. Two pages
answer most questions:

- [What is and is not established](https://elementmerc.github.io/senbonzakura/guide/what-we-know),
  if your question is whether a figure means what it looks like it means.
- [Install](https://elementmerc.github.io/senbonzakura/guide/install), if the tool will not run.

`senbonzakura doctor` checks that your install can actually do what it claims: the pinned tools,
the architecture modules, the bundled data. Its output is the single most useful thing to paste
into a question.

## What makes a question easy to answer

The command you ran, what you expected, what happened, and the output of `senbonzakura doctor`.
If a number is the problem, the result file matters more than the number: the file carries the
conditions the figure was taken under, and the conditions are usually the answer.

**Do not paste a model's generations, and do not attach a harmful prompt set.** Neither belongs
in a public thread, and neither is needed to diagnose anything. Describe the behaviour instead.
`CONTRIBUTING.md` explains why this is a firm rule rather than a preference.

## What to expect

One maintainer, working on this alongside other things. Questions usually get an answer within a
week. A question that sits longer has been missed rather than declined, so it is fine to bump it.

There is no commercial support and no service level attached to any of this.
